# Final Research Position

The Ancestor Architecture proposes:

\[
\boxed{
The ultimate measure of intelligence is not the ability to produce correct answers.
}
\]

It is:

\[
\boxed{
The ability to create systems that continue producing better answers after the original intelligence is gone.
}
\]

An intelligence reaches its highest form when it becomes a bridge between generations of discovery.

Not a final authority.

Not a permanent controller.

A generator of successors.
