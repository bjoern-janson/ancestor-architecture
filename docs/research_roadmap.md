# Ancestor Architecture: Research Roadmap

## Purpose

This document defines a progression from conceptual framework to empirical validation.

The goal is not to prove the framework by analogy, but to identify measurable mechanisms that determine whether systems become:

- dependency engines
- optimization engines
- adaptive catalysts
- ancestor systems

The central research question:

\[
\boxed{
Can an intelligence increase future intelligence without becoming the center of it?
}
\]

---
